# QSAG assurance and deployment boundary

QSAG combines SLH-DSA and ML-DSA signing, threshold custody, pure-quintic Hem proofs and classical-to-post-quantum key migration. It is a research prototype with no external audit. The evidence below identifies the formal statements, implementation artifacts and deployment assumptions behind each claim.

## Signature equivalence and custody isolation

Successful threshold and reference evaluations have byte-identical signatures on matched keys, parameters, messages and randomness for the schemes modeled in `Top/Signing`. The four-hop reduction gives bounds on the signing game conditional on the supplied `SigningPrimitiveBounds`, including primitive advantage and query limits for the chosen suite. Instantiating the numerical budget establishes its size. Instantiating those premises for the deployed hash is a separate cryptographic obligation. [Signing refinements](lean/Qsag/Top/Signing.lean) · [Production bounds](lean/Qsag/Protocol/Signing/ProductionJortsBaggyBounds.lean)

  - **`PersonalReleaseParty::ceremony_over_wire` & Dealerless MSP DKG (`crates/qsag-quorum/src/personal_release/`):** Supports both trusted-dealer and **dealerless wire enrollment** (`run_dealerless_personal_release_dkg_over_wire` in `personal_release/dkg.rs`). `Custody/CommitteeHandoffAndDealerlessDkg.lean` proves aggregate column uniformity (`dealerless_sum_uniform`), singleton-span rejection (`rejects_singleton_span`), exact secret privacy via kernel fiber bijections (`secret_view_fibers`, alongside `full_vector_uniform_given_row_impossible` documenting why corrupt mask-row views require a privacy witness), and 3-of-5 / 4-of-5 `CommitteeHandoffJorts` lease safety.

## Cryptographic profiles and protocol boundaries

### 1. Public aggregation, zero knowledge and recursive IVC

The extension is $\mathbb F_{p^5}=\mathbb F_p[X]/(X^5-3)$ for $p=2^{64}-2^{32}+1$, with cardinality greater than $2^{319.99}$. Frobenius acts by $X^p=\zeta_5 X$, where $3^{(p-1)/5}\bmod p=\zeta_5=1{,}041{,}288{,}259{,}238{,}279{,}555$. [Field implementation](crates/qsag-agg/src/zk_hem/v2/field5.rs)

- **Public committees.** `prove_hem_quintic_128` and `verify_hem_quintic_128` implement `HemQuintic128` (`QSAGHQM2`). The four-key, three-active vector is 360,391 B with `HemQuinticProfile::TurboShake400` (400-bit Canopy) and 381,661 B with `HemQuinticProfile::Poseidon2Gl24` (448-bit, seven-limb Canopy). Inactive padding pins all 138 non-S-box columns to canonical zero. Stage 0 takes 267 queries at $2^{16}$ through $2^{23}$ cells and 266 at $2^{24}$ through $2^{28}$. For both profiles `publicHemQuintic_budget_lt_128` evaluates the public error formula below $2^{-128}$ for all 13 schedules through $Q\le 2^{64}$. [Public certificate](lean/Qsag/Protocol/Aggregation/PublicHemQuintic128.lean) · [Row constraints](crates/qsag-agg/src/hem_quintic/rows.rs)
- **Regional aggregation.** `HierarchicalHemAggregate` (`QSAGHEM\x04`) includes the public proofs for its regional shards. `prove_hierarchical_hem_prepared` and `verify_hierarchical_hem_prepared` bind them to an independently prepared `PreparedPolicyRegistry`. [Registry and verifier](crates/qsag-agg/src/recursive.rs)
- **Zero knowledge.** zk-Hem v2 uses `QSAGZKH2` locally and `QSAGZKS2` for additive shares. Both wire decoders accept cSHAKE256 (16) and TurboSHAKE256 (17). Stage 0 commits a packed assignment and a folded-domain mask word, with five out-of-domain masking points, a 32-byte per-tree `tree_nonce` and 32-byte per-leaf salts. Optional `LookupV2Proof` adds a jointly masked LogUp helper over `Fp5`. The [CWYZ26] affine folds and [DKT26] Theorem 5.12 line-folding analysis underlie `zkHemV2_packed_all13_budget_lt_128` for all 13 deployed sizes. [Wire profile decoder](crates/qsag-agg/src/zk_hem/v2/params.rs) · [Packed-fiber certificate](lean/Qsag/Protocol/Aggregation/ZkHemV2PackedFiberCerts.lean)
- **Binary recursive IVC.** `prove_ivc_tree` and `verify_ivc_tree` implement `SuccinctIvcProof` (`QSAGIVC1`, strict tag `0x00`). `IvcTreeStepRelation` checks both `FixedPathWhirW24` (`QSAGIW24`) child openings over Poseidon2-GL-w24 and `Fp5`. A degree-two sumcheck reduces public-matrix claims to one at the root. The native two-child census is 315,150 rows including closing padding, within $2^{19}$. The full [DKT26] step uses 7,652,354 rows before closing and fits within $2^{23}$. Root size is 639,673 B independent of depth (`ivcRootProof_byteSize_independent_of_depth`). A fixed depth-two root and its child stitch pass kernel evaluation with a fixture oracle. The separate four-leaf execution uses a different oracle and proof. The executable test accepts the root, finds no collision in 1,057 transcript entries and extracts leaves 0, 1, 2 and 3. The kernel theorem takes that Boolean success as a premise. `multidepth_recursive_budget_lt_128` bounds modeled error below $2^{-128}$ up to depth 16 (65,536 shards) at $Q\le 2^{64}$, with zero $2^d$ rewinding loss. That numerical theorem uses the $2^{20}$-cell recursive margin schedule (304, 148 and 100 queries). The serialized Rust root uses `IvcStepConfig::dkt26(23)`. For 23 variables, `depth16Schedule_tree_lt_128` bounds the sum of the public sumchecks, packed WHIR terms for every node, and shared Merkle term at depth 16 and $Q\le 2^{64}$ using stage counts 284, 141, 94 and 71 at DKT multiplicity 54. The Rust two-child circuit census for these counts is 7,977,424 rows and 8,106,781 columns including closing. Both fit in $2^{23}$. The 320/156/110/86 alternative exceeds capacity in both dimensions. Rust uses the original DKT26 schedule. A profile change would alter the wire geometry and transcript. Applying the bound to the Rust verifier also requires a reduction from failed child-witness extraction to the counted events. [Binary IVC](crates/qsag-agg/src/zk_hem/recursive_ivc/) · [Tree extraction](lean/Qsag/Protocol/Aggregation/MultiDepthRecursiveQromExtractor.lean)

### 2. Dishonest-majority blinded MAC checks, active Beaver pool sacrifice, and `SoftSpokenOT`
- **Active Beaver sacrifice on all pooled blinding triples (`mac_blind.rs` and the personal release module):** `OtMesh::refill_blind_pool` (`Tower128`), `OtMesh::refill_ext2_blind_pool` (`GoldilocksExt2`), and `PartyOtEndpoint::prestage_ext2_blinding_inner` generate companion sacrifice triples, run two-round echo-committed random linear checks before sealing any triple into the pool (`< 2^{-255}` soundness per staged triple), and permanently poison/zeroize the pool on mismatch (`31 µs` online check).
- **Interactive `SoftSpokenOT (n_C = 256, k = 2)` (`RLWE_BASE_OT_COUNT = 512`):** `Protocol/Threshold/SoftSpokenAndToprfConnections.lean` proves `softspoken_consistency_simulation_budget` ($2^{-241} + B^2 / 2^{256}$, strictly $< 2^{-128}$ iff $B < 2^{64}$) for the 128-slice consistency observation and universal-hash collision; full malicious subspace-VOLE sender/receiver view composition and correlation-robustness reductions follow Roy (CRYPTO 2022, Thm. 4.5).

### 3. TOPRF/BlindVC, `UnlinkableCapabilityRelation`, and 19-kind Zcash/multi-chain migration
- **TOPRF.** In the Shamir-SPDZ arithmetic model, accepted evaluation gives the reference OPRF value, a MAC forgery, or cancellation of the honest blinding randomizer. The finite forgery game has bound $1/p^2$ for a uniform secret MAC key, valid server/session material and a fixed value-sharing distribution across keys. The model covers the arithmetic of `voprf_spdz`. Refinement of the full Rust credential service remains open. [Arithmetic refinement](lean/Qsag/Protocol/Credentials/ToprfSpdzArithmeticRefinement.lean)
- **BlindVC.** Issuance uses 438 repetitions of three-party Goldilocks MPC-in-the-head [IKOS07, GMO16]. Selective-disclosure presentations, private multisignatures and unlinkable capabilities use zk-Hem v2. [Issuance](crates/qsag-cred/src/blind_vc.rs) · [Presentation relation](crates/qsag-agg/src/zk_hem/blind_vc.rs)
- **Key migration.** `qsag-migrate` implements local and collaborative zk-Hem v2 ownership proofs for 19 CLI kinds across Bitcoin/Taproot/Ethereum secp256k1, Solana Ed25519, BLS12-381 and Zcash NU7 Sapling, Orchard, ZIP-2005 Ironwood and ZIP-2007 Transparent. `ClassicalCommitmentKind` and `ClassicalOwnershipWitness` provide the classical commitment and witness types. `hem::collab::Engine::new_anchored` burns `tape_id()` against an `ExternalMonotoneAnchor` before round 1. Each Toom-3 shared `Fp5` multiplication consumes nine base-field triples. [Classical primitives](crates/qsag-migrate/src/classical.rs) · [Collaborative prover](crates/qsag-migrate/src/hem/collab/)

## Specification boundaries

| Interface | Checked statement | Deployment assumption or open obligation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signing | Latched-machine refinement and reductions to primitive games. | The `*_of_primitive_bounds` theorems consume hardness assumptions on the chosen suite. |
| Custody | An approved fresh epoch lies between the watermark and its inclusive window limit. WAL replay checks the old binding. Public approval receipts carry party, deployment and intent information. The monotone velocity model excludes repeated approved nonces on extracted traces. | Reconstruction consumes a private contribution. Channel privacy, MAC key provisioning and durable storage semantics are deployment assumptions. The distinct nonce-set policy accepts some requests the monotone guard rejects. |
| Credentials | The authenticated verifier supplies the clock. A presentation checks its challenge against that verifier’s stored time, and a backward clock update cannot revive an expired challenge. Accepted presentations consume the nonce. | Environment progress determines clock progress. A concrete service must implement the clock and the authentication contract. |
| Migration | The ideal ledger conserves value and excludes duplicate nullifiers. A complete group-law witness exists for the order-31 subgroup of `secp7Mod43` over the field of 43 elements. | Production curve results quantify over group laws. Prime moduli alone do not establish those laws. Relating the production circuit’s scalar wires and every signature branch to the modeled ownership witness remains open. |
| Aggregation | Public and packed error formulas evaluate below $2^{-128}$ for all 13 sizes at $Q\le 2^{64}$. Reduced one- and three-stage quintic verifiers have constructed round certificates. | The deployed soundness reduction needs a certificate for its actual environment, schedule and challenge width. A scalar 400-bit seed selects position zero in every packed query. Full-width packed positions have separate counting proofs. |
| Random oracle | Lazy and uniform permutation transcript distributions coincide in the finite game. | Instantiating the random permutation or oracle with concrete Keccak or Poseidon2 requires an additional cryptographic assumption. |

The paired-MAC equations in the threshold model support its algebraic forgery bound. The production eight-component `BatchMacTag` has no end-to-end MIR refinement in this audit.

The archive has functional equivalence proofs for 26 of 28 symbols, including the original group of 12. The AVX2 and NEON transpose routines remain open. Conditional instruction-trace independence covers 19 x86-64 and 9 AArch64 symbols. The x86 composition fixes the public initial state specified in its theorem. The hardware timing claim assumes DOIT or DIT as appropriate. The parser binds the fixture bytes to the decoded symbols. Matching those fixtures to a shipped executable requires an external build and relocation check. The GFNI and PCLMUL comparisons with extracted MIR use a block count of one.
