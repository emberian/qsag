# QSAG assurance and deployment boundary

`qsag` (**Quorum-Switching AGgregation / Quantum Succinct AGgregation**) combines standardized post-quantum signature formats (`FIPS 205 SLH-DSA`, `FIPS 204 ML-DSA`) with custom Cut-the-Hash threshold custody, `[CWYZ26]` affine-combiner WHIR aggregation (`Hem`), four-curve collaborative key migration (`CoZK`), and synthesizable verifier/enclave RTL (`QSAG-NI`, `Dregg-180`). This document separates machine-checked statements about specific artifacts from the integration, refinement, and physical-security obligations required in deployment. **Status: research prototype; no external audit has been performed.**

## Signature equivalence and custody isolation

- **Matched-input byte identity:** For **FIPS 205 SLH-DSA-SHAKE**, **Baggy**, **Jorts 2-of-3**, **isolated CNF SLH-DSA/Baggy**, and **QS-B (`BftShamirJorts`)**, successful threshold evaluations produce byte-for-byte identical signatures to a standalone signer given the **same secret key, parameters, leaf/message, and hedging seed/randomizer** (statistical distance `0` under coupled coins). Conditional on malicious abort or independent per-party entropy policies, output distributions require separate conditioning analysis. Threshold **FIPS 204 ML-DSA-44/65/87** outputs verify under unmodified standard verifiers; transcript privacy of rejected signing attempts (`(w1, reject)` opened while `z` stays closed) relies on the threshold Fiat–Shamir-with-aborts heuristic.
- **Separated wire custody vs. in-process engines (`qsag-quorum`, `qsag-core`):**
  - **`IsolatedCnfParty<T: WireTransport, J: PoisonJournal>` (`crates/qsag-quorum/src/cnf_party.rs`)** executes genuine separated honest-majority `(t, n)` ($2(t-1) < n$: `2-of-3`, `3-of-5`, `4-of-7`, ...) Cut-the-Hash signing for both `Baggy` and standard `SLH-DSA` over `MacSealedWireChannel` (Chida et al. CRYPTO 2018 dual `[r * w]` multiplication verification in $\mathrm{GF}(2^{128})$, statistical soundness $\approx 2^{-126}$ per barrier, 2-round `QSAG.R_COMMIT/v1` hedged coin tossing). No party or coordinator ever sees another party's CNF lanes.
  - **`PersonalReleaseParty::ceremony_over_wire` (`crates/qsag-quorum/src/personal_release.rs`)** executes Cachin–Tackmann asymmetric quorum intersection ($B^3$), `PersonalBeltLatch` certification, local 0/1 `MonotoneSpanProgram` collapse, and encrypted wire SPDZ (`Π_MAC` + 118 `Π_pow7` tuples), opening only the Poseidon2 PRF-check digest. Note that `deal_personal_release_committee` currently mints initial MSP shares via a trusted dealer ceremony; dealerless asymmetric DKG is future work.
  - **`CnfRssProjectedEngine` (`crates/qsag-core/src/cnf_rss.rs`)** and local multi-share test harnesses hold multiple shares in one address space and are simulation/benchmark drivers, **not** separated custody deployments.
- **QS-A vs. QS-B vs. Blocklace anchoring:** **QS-A** (`CommitteePartitionedJorts`) partitions leaves as `committee * leaves_per_committee + epoch`, revealing which committee signed and allowing $t$-of-$t$ online signing with abort. **QS-B** shares one global tree across up to 255 custodians under $2q > n + f$ (symmetric availability when $q \le n - f$, i.e., $n \ge 3f + 1$). **`BlocklaceLeafAnchor`** (`crates/qsag-core/src/jorts.rs`, 120 bytes) binds single-signer or grassroots blocklace sequence numbers monotonically to Jorts leaf indices (`seq == leaf_idx`), turning any blocklace equivocation into a publicly verifiable 8,800–9,440-byte `JortsEquivocationEvidence` slashing proof.

## Cryptographic profiles and protocol boundaries

### 1. Baggy, Jorts, and FTS
- **Baggy (`PORS+FP`):** `w = 4, chains = 128, target_sum = 192` `WOTS+C` over a single 2,048-leaf pruned tree opening 28 leaves (`<= 128` multiproof nodes, `<= 71,654` B; legacy `FORS+C` is removed). `Protocol/Signing/PorsFpSecurity.lean` proves canonical multiproof binding and exact subset-cover bounds after $g = 1..4$ signatures on one bottom tree: `209.788`, `157.024`, `136.093`, and `122.451` bits (establishing that Rust's `forgery_bits_milli` is strictly conservative at all four points).
- **FTS (`qsag-fts`):** Production profiles use 32-byte digests only (`Compact1122` at 1,122 B, `w = 96, h = 8, L = 34`, and `Full256Bit` at 2,338 B, `w = 5, L = 72`; 16-byte profiles are deleted). A concrete `w = 96` EUF-CMA reduction for `Compact1122` remains open. In the dealerless threshold FTS ceremony (`crates/qsag-fts/src/dkg.rs`), active consistency checks are backed by fault-injection tests rather than a Lean proof, selective failure on $k$ OT mask choice bits occurs with probability $2^{-k}$ (touching mask/MAC-key choices rather than signing-key lanes), and the CLI exposes the 8-leaf `--fast-demo` ceremony.

Capacity-proximity wire tags `0, 1, 3` (`Habr24`) are retired and rejected by wire decoders (`crates/qsag-agg/src/pcs.rs`). - **Query-accounting vs. composed QROM boundary:** Tags `5–8, 10` satisfy `meets_bounded_qrom_128_profile` (also aliased by historical helper `meets_qrom_128_floor`) under `[CDHZ25]` state-restoration accounting up to $2^{38}$ or $2^{40}$ queries and make no claim at $2^{64}$ queries. In Lean (`Protocol/Aggregation/ClosedWhirSchedule.lean`), `singleStageExactScheduleCert` is discharged unconditionally (`closed_whir_single_stage_qrom_bound`), whereas the 4-stage `deployed_whir_exec_closed_qrom_128` theorem still takes a `WhirScheduleCert` hypothesis and `whirAffineStageSimpleNum` is not yet bridged to `[CWYZ26]`'s `cwyz26AffineLineError`.
- **Subtree recursion modes:**
  1. `SuccinctSubtreeAggregate` (`zk_hem/recursive_whir.rs`) proves up to 16 child PCS openings in public mode (`prove_public`). `verify_succinct_shard_whir_merge` requires accompanying `VerifiedHierarchicalHemEvidence` (`check_succinct_shard_whir_merge` checks PCS openings only).
  2. Contractive binary recursion (`SplitSubtreeAggregate` + `FoldedLevelWhirProof`, `crates/qsag-agg/src/recursive/split.rs`) verifies two child Spark/LogUp-GKR hologram IOPs inside $\le 2^{20}$ rows (`1,048,576` at depth 2; `524,288` at depth 3), commits child `DeferredPcsClaim` digests into `accumulator_root`, and folds all homogeneous claims at each tree level via `[CWYZ26]` into one `FoldedLevelWhirProof` per level, verified through depth 3 on CPU and RX 6750 XT Vulkan. End-to-end multi-level QROM knowledge extraction for recursive transcripts is not yet mechanized in `Spec/IdealAggregate.lean`. In masked zero-knowledge mode (`BlindVC`, `zk-Multisig`), one Reed–Solomon encoder scratch buffer is currently dropped without explicit zeroization.

### 3. Dishonest-majority blinded MAC checks and pooled `OtMesh`
Opening an unblinded SPDZ residual $d = m - \alpha x$ on an invalid share leaks $-\alpha \Delta$ when $t = n$ or during polynomial resharing. `crates/qsag-core/src/preprocessing/mac_blind.rs` eliminates all unblinded openings across Goldilocks ($\mathbb{F}_{p^2}$, false-zero $p^{-2}$), `Tower128` (two rounds, $2^{-256}$), and additive Boolean openings ($< 2^{-256}$): residuals enter KOS-OLE strictly as sender inputs against disposable receiver choice masks, and parties commit all shares of $w = (\sum_i r_i)(\sum_j d_j)$ before opening $w$.
- **Lean coverage (`Protocol/Threshold/MacBlind.lean`):** Proves `macBlind_abort_independent_of_alpha`, `macBlind_spdz_alpha_zero_leakage`, `macBlind_tower128_two_round_256bit_sound`, and `three_projection_mac_blind_bound` in the **ideal-OLE hybrid model**. Concrete computational instantiation of KOS OT + PRG + MASCOT leftover-hash correlation against `MacBlind.lean` remains outside the Lean proof.
- **Pooled online execution (`OtMesh`):** `OtMesh` retains pre-generated single-use Tower-field OLE blinding triples `(r, a, c = r·a)` with strictly monotone serial numbers, reducing 3-party online MAC checks from `13.75 ms` to **`31 µs`** (`4.04 ms` amortized offline refill). Any MAC check failure permanently poisons and zeroizes the pool.

### 4. RLWE preprocessing, TOPRF/BlindVC, and 4-curve `CoZK` migration
- **RLWE preprocessing:** 24 challenges in $\{\pm X^k : 0 \le k < 1024\}$ enforce both $\|z\|_\infty \le 3070$ and $\|z_s\|_2^2 + \|z_e\|_2^2 \le 1{,}500{,}000{,}000$ (`Math/CyclotomicRlwe.lean` and `Protocol/Threshold/RlweExtraction.lean` prove `extract_rlwe_or_sigma_24_euclidean_sis_dichotomy` and `tooth_rlweUnitSis_refuted_by_euclidean_bound`, refuting the unit public solution $s = 1, e = \mathrm{centered}(-A)$). Q192 Bernoulli finite-precision tail bounds are not yet connected to the production sampler.
- **TOPRF and BlindVC (`qsag-cred`):** `voprf.rs` (`rss`, 2-of-5 replicated) requires **all 5 servers online** during evaluation so majority voting can correct 1 malicious fault; `voprf_spdz.rs` (`shamir-spdz`, $2 \le t \le n \le 64$) evaluates with any $t$ servers online over a non-cloneable monotone preprocessing reservoir (`Refusal::Request` burns one reservation; `Refusal::Quorum` poisons the reservoir and wipes MAC shares). `BlindVC` supports 438-round ZKBoo issuance and either 33-round 256-party $\mathrm{GF}(p^5)$ MPC-in-the-head presentation ($256^{-33} = 2^{-264}$ leading term; exact interactive bound $< 2^{-263}$ proved in `Protocol/Credentials/RepetitionCutAndChoose.lean`) or `239,507`-byte zk-Hem presentation (tag 7).
- **Four-curve `CoZK` migration (`qsag-migrate`):** Local and threshold collaborative ZKBoo (`CoZkCustodianShare`: 3-custodian `2-of-3` replicated `outer_mpc` and general `(t, n)` MAC-authenticated `threshold_mpc` / `threshold_prep`) covers **`secp256k1`** (P2PKH, P2WPKH, Taproot, Ethereum), **`Ed25519`** (SLIP-0010), **`Pallas`** (Orchard direct and rerandomized), and **`BLS12-381`** (`0x00` validator withdrawal credentials) at 438 repetitions (`(2/3)^438 < 2^-256` classical, `128.1` nominal Grover bits) with `TurboSHAKE256` view commitments (`COZK_VIEW_MAGIC_V4`) and active pre-open zero tests (`Protocol/Migration/ActiveMigrationZeroTest.lean`: `rustBitCheckStages_sound`; Boolean flush bound $< 2^{-127.99}$ in general MPC / $< 2^{-244}$ squared batch, prime-field `< 2^-249`, cubic Goldilocks `< 2^-186`). Rejection-sampled field tapes have zero modular bias; retry exhaustion causes liveness abort.

## Durable storage and real hardware anti-rollback anchors

- **Paged journals and sector-tear recovery:** `qsag-core`, `qsag-fts` (`QSBURN05`), and `qsag-hd` (`QSHDQB04`) place genesis on page 0, the two high-water-mark slots on separate 4,096-byte pages (`4096` and `8192`), and append records from `12288` through `qsag_asm::durable::LockedRecord` (`flock` bound to `OpenDescriptionId` with `fstat` mode check `mode & 0o077 == 0`). `Exec/PosixCrashWal.lean` models POSIX writeback/NVMe crashes, and `Exec/TwoSlotHwmCrashModel.lean` proves both separate-page monotonicity and uncommitted-tail truncation recovery (`append_tear_truncate_recovers`), matching production Rust recovery.
- **Hardware and hypervisor anti-rollback anchors (`crates/qsag-core/src/anti_rollback.rs`):** Whole-disk or VM snapshot restoration cannot be detected by a local WAL alone. Contrary to earlier drafts, `anti_rollback.rs` implements **three production external anchors in `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]` Rust** (alongside deterministic test doubles):
  1. **`Tpm2NvMonotoneAnchor` (`--anchor-tpm-nv <idx>`):** Marshals ISO/IEC 11889 `TPM2_NV_ReadPublic`, `TPM2_NV_Increment`, and `TPM2_NV_Read` frames directly over `/dev/tpmrm0` (requiring `TPMA_NV_ORDERLY == 0` so every increment persists to TPM NVRAM), bound to a local two-phase `(committed, pending)` recovery seal that absorbs crash-window increments without false-bricking. Note: if Password-Auth is used without parameter encryption, an active root interposer on `/dev/tpmrm0` could spoof `NV_Read` replies; high-assurance hosts should pair TPM NV indices with HMAC sessions or `Dregg-180` SPI FRAM custody.
  2. **`VmGenIdForkGuard` / `HypervisorGenerationAnchor` (`--anchor-vmgenid`):** Samples `/sys/devices/virtual/misc/vmgenid` (16-byte hypervisor generation UUID), `/proc/sys/kernel/random/boot_id`, and `(st_dev, st_ino, st_ctime)` continuity before and after every ceremony.
  3. **`FsDirectoryMonotoneAnchor` / `FsExternalMountAnchor` (`--anchor-dir <dir>`):** Enforces disjoint `st_dev` filesystem mounts (unless `--allow-single-disk-anchor` is explicitly passed) with single-use `AnchorTicket` seals (`QSAGANC1`).
- **CLI enforcement coverage:** Stateful vaults in `keygen`, `sign`, `aggregate`, `quorum` (`keygen`, `sign`, `refresh`, `reshare`), `quorum hem`, `cred` (`voprf-keygen` and credential vaults), `hd`, `policy init`, and `policy` capability ledgers require `--anchor-dir`, `--anchor-tpm-nv`, or `--anchor-vmgenid`—or the explicit disposable waiver `--ephemeral-unanchored` (never both, and an anchored vault refuses `--ephemeral-unanchored`). Volatile coupons (`quorum coupon`, `Custody/VolatileCoupon.lean`), the `--fast-demo` dealerless FTS ceremony, and stateless `migrate` proofs do not take an external anchor.
