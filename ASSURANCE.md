# QSAG assurance and deployment boundary

`qsag` (**Quorum-Switching AGgregation / Quantum Succinct AGgregation**) combines standardized post-quantum signature formats (`FIPS 205 SLH-DSA`, `FIPS 204 ML-DSA`) with Cut-the-Hash threshold custody, `[CWYZ26]` / `[DKT26]` affine-combiner WHIR aggregation (`Hem`, `HemQuintic128`, `zk-Hem v2`, and `QSAGIVC1`), multi-ecosystem post-quantum key migration (`zk-Hem v2`), and synthesizable / placed-and-routed verifier and enclave silicon (`QSAG-NI v2`, `ASS2-0P5 / Dregg-64`, and `CL_QSAG_F2` for AWS EC2 F2 `VU47P`; superseded `Dregg-180` and academic PDK prototypes are archived in [`history/`](history/README.md)). This document separates machine-checked statements about specific artifacts from the integration, refinement, and physical-security obligations required in deployment. **Status: research prototype; no external audit has been performed.**

## Signature equivalence and custody isolation

- **Matched-input byte identity & 4-hop `< 2^{-128}` EUF-CMA:** For **FIPS 205 SLH-DSA-SHAKE**, **Baggy**, **Jorts 2-of-3**, **isolated CNF SLH-DSA/Baggy**, and **QS-B (`BftShamirJorts`)**, successful threshold evaluations produce byte-for-byte identical signatures to a standalone signer on matched key, parameters, leaf/message, and hedging seed/randomizer. `Protocol/Signing/FullAdaptiveEufCma.lean` removes the legacy `hCand` `pk`-independence restriction; `HulsingMultiHopReduction.lean` mechanizes the 4-hop Hülsing reduction (`PRF_msg -> PRF -> SM-DT-UD -> ITSR / SM-DT-TCR / SM-DT-PRE`), and `ProductionJortsBaggyBounds.lean` discharges concrete `< 2^{-128}` classical and QROM bounds for `Jorts` ($q_s \le 2^{16}$), `Baggy`, `Corduroy`, and `FIPS 205 SLH-DSA-SHAKE-128s/128f` ($q_s = 2^{64}$), with probability-`1` `#tooth_pair` refutations when `BeltLatch`, `checkTargetSum`, or `HashAdrs` layer/keypair separation is disabled.
- **Separated wire custody, dealerless enrollment, and deployment binding (`qsag-quorum`, `qsag-core`):**
  - **`IsolatedCnfParty<T: WireTransport, J: PoisonJournal>` (`crates/qsag-quorum/src/cnf_party.rs`)** executes genuine separated honest-majority `(t, n)` ($2(t-1) < n$) Cut-the-Hash signing over `MacSealedWireChannel` bound to a 32-byte `deployment_id` (`QSAG.CUSTODY.DEPLOYMENT/v2`). `Custody/IsolatedCnfTraceAndCoinBinding.lean` proves fixed-width 15-step wire-shape key/digit noninterference (`isolatedCnf_fixedWidth_wireShape_keyIndependent`), and `Top/DistributedAuthRunBound.lean` proves deployment-bound authenticated ingress (`distributed_authRun_adaptive_unsafe_release_bound`).
  - **`PersonalReleaseParty::ceremony_over_wire` & Dealerless MSP DKG (`crates/qsag-quorum/src/personal_release/`):** Supports both trusted-dealer and **dealerless wire enrollment** (`run_dealerless_personal_release_dkg_over_wire` in `personal_release/dkg.rs`). `Custody/CommitteeHandoffAndDealerlessDkg.lean` proves aggregate column uniformity (`dealerless_sum_uniform`), singleton-span rejection (`rejects_singleton_span`), exact secret privacy via kernel fiber bijections (`secret_view_fibers`, alongside `full_vector_uniform_given_row_impossible` documenting why corrupt mask-row views require a privacy witness), and 3-of-5 / 4-of-5 `CommitteeHandoffJorts` lease safety.

## Cryptographic profiles and protocol boundaries

### 1. Hem: legacy `Ext4` (`WhirSecurityRegime`), public `HemQuintic128` (`QSAGHQM2`), and `zk-Hem v2` (`[DKT26]`)
- **Legacy `Ext4` (`WhirSecurityRegime` tags `4–10`, 256-bit commitments):** Retained for backward compatibility and GPU benchmarks; composed through BCS, 256-bit Merkle/transcript digests cap composed knowledge soundness at **127 bits classically and 81 bits in the QROM** (`crates/qsag-agg/SECURITY.md`; tag `2` is classical test-only and rejected by aggregate verifiers; capacity tags `0, 1, 3` are rejected).
- **128-bit QROM public aggregation (`HemQuintic128`, wire `QSAGHQM2`, CLI `--hem-profile quintic-turbo | quintic-gl24`):** Uses $\mathbb{F}_{p^5}$ challenges ($p^5 \approx 2^{320}$), 400-bit `TurboSHAKE256` (`362,391 B` sparse-committee vector) or 7-limb 448-bit `Poseidon2-GL-w24` (`381,045 B`) commitments, and `[DKT26]` Stage-0 query counts (`267` for $2^{16}..2^{23}$, `266` for $2^{24}..2^{28}$). `Protocol/Aggregation/PublicHemQuintic128.lean` (`publicHemQuintic_qrom_soundness_128`) proves modeled QROM error `< 2^{-128}` (`~129.19` bits at $2^{24}$) across all 13 sizes for all $Q \le 2^{64}$.
- **Committed-mask `zk-Hem v2` (`QSAGZKH2` / `QSAGZKS2`, tags `16–19`) & all 13 packed-fiber certificates:** Uses 32-byte per-tree `tree_nonce`, 32-byte per-leaf Stage-0 salts, full-rank LogUp joint masking (`LookupPoleHit` public retry), degree-4 helper kernel clamp, `SecretScratch`/`SecretBuffer` zeroization on RS/WHIR scratch buffers, and schedule-bound coin derivation (`Custody/IsolatedCnfTraceAndCoinBinding.lean`: `proverCoins_schedule_non_reuse`, `v2Stream_reuse_implies_collision`). `Protocol/Aggregation/ZkHemV2ComposedZkAndCerts.lean` mechanizes the LogUp joint-masking bijection (`lookup_disclosure_with_retries`) and Stage-0 projected four-view algebraic simulator (`all_fold_projected_four_view_sim`, plus `shared_polynomial_views_obstruct_full_sim` proving why full quotient remainders stay private). `Protocol/Aggregation/ZkHemV2PackedFiberCerts.lean` (`zkHemV2_packed_all13_qrom_lt_128_at_2pow64`) proves `packedStageDepth = logDomain`, the `< 2^{-277}` single-OOD Guruswami–Sudan bad-seed envelope across all 45 stage entries, and unconditional `< 2^{-128}` QROM certificates for **all 13 deployed schedules (`16 <= logCells <= 28`) at $Q \le 2^{64}$** across both `TurboShake400` and `Poseidon2-GL-w24`.
- **Subtree recursion & multi-depth straight-line QROM extraction:**
  1. `SuccinctIvcProof` (`QSAGIVC1`, `crates/qsag-agg/src/recursive_ivc/`): Constant-byte-size depth-independent binary subtree recursion over `Poseidon2-GL-w24` and $\mathbb{F}_{p^5}$, folding child public-matrix claims via a degree-2 sumcheck so root proof byte length is strictly independent of tree depth (`26,185 B` measured identically across depths $1, 2, 3$ / `2, 4, 8` shards, `209,480/209,480` bit-flips rejected; `639,673 B` in the $2^{23}$-cell `[DKT26]` production geometry).
  2. `Protocol/Aggregation/MultiDepthRecursiveQromExtractor.lean` proves `ivcRootProof_byteSize_independent_of_depth`, `poseidon2W24_twoChildStep_fits_2pow20_rows` (`315,150 <= 2^{19}` native $x^7$ / `855,590 <= 2^{20}` degree-3 rows, paired with `tooth_poseidon2_w12_twoChildStep_exceeds_2pow20_rows`), and the inductive straight-line QROM tree-extraction reduction (`extractMultiDepthQrom_inductive_sound`, `multidepth_recursive_qrom_lt_2_neg_128`: zero $2^d$ rewinding loss on $Q$, `< 2^{-128}` up to $d \le 16$ / `65,536` shards at $Q \le 2^{64}$). **Current specification boundary (tracked in [`docs/MIL_STD_ASSURANCE_MATRIX.md`](docs/MIL_STD_ASSURANCE_MATRIX.md)):** `SuccinctIvcEnv` currently parameterizes step acceptance (`whirStepAccepts`) and QROM witness lookup (`lookupNodeWitness`) as abstract callbacks, and `SuccinctWhirExtractorAndIdealAggregate.lean` instantiates `AggregateExtractorSound` on a 1-stage 64-element degree-1 base schedule rather than the 4-stage `[CWYZ26]`/`[DKT26]` folding IOP; instantiating `SuccinctIvcEnv` directly with the executable R1CS + 4-stage WHIR verifier is an active EAL7/MIL-STD-498 specification obligation.

### 2. Dishonest-majority blinded MAC checks, active Beaver pool sacrifice, and `SoftSpokenOT`
- **Active Beaver sacrifice on all pooled blinding triples (`mac_blind.rs`, `personal_release.rs`):** `OtMesh::refill_blind_pool` (`Tower128`), `OtMesh::refill_ext2_blind_pool` (`GoldilocksExt2`), and `PartyOtEndpoint::prestage_ext2_blinding_inner` generate companion sacrifice triples, run two-round echo-committed random linear checks before sealing any triple into the pool (`< 2^{-255}` soundness per staged triple), and permanently poison/zeroize the pool on mismatch (`31 µs` online check).
- **Interactive `SoftSpokenOT (n_C = 256, k = 2)` (`RLWE_BASE_OT_COUNT = 512`):** `Protocol/Threshold/SoftSpokenAndToprfConnections.lean` proves `softspoken_consistency_simulation_budget` ($2^{-241} + B^2 / 2^{256}$, strictly $< 2^{-128}$ iff $B < 2^{64}$) for the 128-slice consistency observation and universal-hash collision; full malicious subspace-VOLE sender/receiver view composition and correlation-robustness reductions follow Roy (CRYPTO 2022, Thm. 4.5).

### 3. TOPRF/BlindVC, `UnlinkableCapabilityRelation`, and 19-kind Zcash/multi-chain migration

- **Anchored collaborative `zk-Hem v2` migration (`qsag-migrate`):** Unanchored `collab::Engine::new` and `test-dealer` are removed from production builds; every collaborative party entrypoint (`Engine::new_anchored`, `prove_ownership_party_anchored`) requires an `ExternalMonotoneAnchor` burning `tape_id()` before round 1.
